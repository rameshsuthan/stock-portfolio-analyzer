# Path 2: Ad Campaign Performance Analyzer

Help a marketer understand which campaigns and channels are delivering results. Build a dashboard from a campaign CSV to compare spend, clicks, conversions, and ROI across Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, and Twitter.

Suggested stack: Replit \+ Streamlit \+ pandas \+ Plotly. You can use another AI coding assistant or stack if you prefer.

**Build these four views:**

* Overview: key metrics and spend-versus-conversions trends.  
* Platform Comparison: compare performance across advertising channels.  
* Campaign Details: a searchable campaign-level table.  
* Time Series: explore how performance changes over time.

Add global filters for date, channel, audience, and campaign goal. Check that every view updates consistently, metrics show their units, and empty filter results display a clear message.

Document your prompts, bugs, and iterations, then submit the same Week 1 deliverables. If you get stuck, refer to the Ad Campaign Analyzer solution kit in the Solutions for use-cases section below.
